### Post 2: Insight (why companies fail with AI)

```
Most companies try AI the wrong way.

They start with the tool:
"Let's get ChatGPT for everyone."
"Let's add an AI chatbot to the website."

Three months later, nothing has really changed.

The companies that actually get value start somewhere else:
the boring, repetitive work.

Here's the simple test I use to find the best AI opportunities in a company:

1. Is it done every day or every week?
2. Does someone copy information from one place to another?
3. Does it follow rules you could explain to a new employee?
4. Does a mistake cost money or time?

Four "yes" answers = a perfect candidate for automation with AI.

Usual suspects:
• Typing invoices and receipts into a system
• Moving leads from forms/email into a CRM
• Writing the same reports every week
• Answering the same internal questions again and again

Start there. Small, measurable, boring.
That's where AI pays for itself first.

What's the most repetitive task in your company right now? 👇
```
