# 02 — Advertising Yourself: Personal Brand & Social Media Plan

Principle: **build in public**. Every project, client lesson and experiment becomes content.
You're not "marketing" — you're documenting work, which proves you can do it.

---

## 1. Foundations (week 1–2)

**Profiles — same photo, name, headline, and link everywhere:**

- **Headline formula:** `Automation & SaaS Developer | I build automations & custom business software for [niche] | n8n · AI · Next.js · Supabase`
- **Bio:** who you help → result → proof → call to action ("DM 'AUDIT' for a free process audit")
- **Link:** one link hub → website with case studies + booking link
- **Banner:** your offer in one line + a screenshot of a system or workflow you built
- **Featured section (LinkedIn):** demo video, best case study, booking link
- **GitHub profile README:** see `../README.md` in this repo
- **Website:** landing page, 3 case studies, services/pricing ("starting at"), booking calendar, newsletter signup

---

## 2. Platform strategy

Don't be everywhere at once. Start with **LinkedIn (primary)** + **one video channel**, add others later.

| Platform | Role | Format | Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| **LinkedIn** (primary) | Where business owners & agencies buy | Text posts, carousels (PDF), short demo videos | 4–5×/week |
| **YouTube** | Trust + search traffic; long-term asset | 8–15 min build tutorials / client-style demos | 1×/week or bi-weekly |
| **Shorts / Reels / TikTok** | Reach | 30–60s demo clips cut from YouTube ("from Excel chaos to one system in 60 seconds") | 3–5×/week (repurposed) |
| **X (Twitter)** | AI builder community, peers, partners | Threads, quick tips, build logs | Daily (short) |
| **Newsletter** (Beehiiv/Substack) | Own your audience | Weekly: 1 build + 1 tool + 1 lesson | Weekly |
| **GitHub** | Proof of skill | Open-source templates, n8n workflows | With each project |
| **Communities** (n8n forum, Reddit, Discord) | Credibility & leads | Answer questions, share templates | 2–3×/week |

If your niche is local (Israel / a specific city), add **Facebook groups & WhatsApp communities** for
business owners — often the highest-converting channel for SMB services.

## 3. Content pillars

Rotate between 5 pillars so you attract both clients and peers:

1. **Demos (sell)** — "Here's the system I built that replaced 6 spreadsheets for a construction company." Show, don't tell.
2. **Case studies & results (prove)** — before/after, numbers, client quotes.
3. **How-to / tutorials (teach)** — n8n workflows, building internal apps, Supabase tips, using AI in automations. Give away the *what*; clients pay for the *done-for-you*.
4. **Opinions & insights (differentiate)** — "Why most AI chatbots fail small businesses", tool comparisons, cost breakdowns.
5. **Behind the scenes (connect)** — freelance journey, wins, mistakes, revenue milestones, how you work.

Mix: ~40% teach, 25% demo, 15% proof, 10% opinion, 10% personal. Every post ends with **one** CTA
(comment a keyword, DM, book a call, subscribe).

---

## 4. Post formats that work

- **Hook → story → lesson → CTA** text posts (first line decides everything)
- **Carousels:** "7 automations every dental clinic should have" (PDF slides, 8–10 pages)
- **Screen-recorded demo** with face cam, 30–90s, captions burned in
- **"I rebuilt X with AI" teardown**
- **Lead magnet posts:** "Comment 'SYSTEM' and I'll send you the n8n template" → collects leads
- **Cost breakdowns:** "Custom system vs. 5 SaaS subscriptions: the real 3-year cost. Here's the math."

Hook templates:
- "Most [niche] businesses still run their business on Excel and WhatsApp. Here's what it costs them: I built a fix in 3 days:"
- "I replaced a 4-hour weekly task with a 12-node n8n workflow. Here's the whole thing:"
- "Stop paying for 7 tools that don't talk to each other. Do this instead:"
- "A client asked me for [X]. I told them no. Here's why:"

## 5. Weekly content system (≈5 hours/week)

| Step | When | What |
|---|---|---|
| Capture | Daily, 2 min | Drop ideas, screenshots, client lessons, voice notes into the idea inbox (agent handles it — see file 03) |
| Plan | Monday 30 min | Agent proposes the week's calendar from ideas + pillars; you approve |
| Create | Monday 2h | Record 1 long video or 2–3 demos; write/approve drafts generated by the agent |
| Repurpose | Agent | 1 long piece → 1 YouTube, 3 shorts, 3 LinkedIn posts, 1 thread, 1 newsletter section |
| Publish | Scheduled | Posting at your audience's peak hours (test; often 8–10am local, Tue–Thu) |
| Engage | Daily 20–30 min | Reply to every comment in the first hour, comment on 10 posts of target clients/peers |
| Review | Friday 20 min | Agent sends analytics report: what worked, what to double down on |

## 6. Turning attention into clients

- **Lead magnets:** n8n templates, "AI readiness checklist for [niche]", prompt packs → email capture
- **Free process audit offer** in bio and pinned post → books discovery calls
- **DM workflow:** someone engages 2–3 times → send a helpful (not salesy) message → offer audit
- **Social proof loop:** every client win → post (with permission) → more inbound
- **Collaborations:** podcast guesting, co-hosted webinars/lives with agencies or tool vendors (n8n, Supabase, CRM and accounting-tool partners)

---

## 7. KPIs to track

| Stage | Metric | Early target (month 3) |
|---|---|---|
| Reach | Impressions / views per week | 10k+ |
| Audience | Followers (LinkedIn), subscribers (YT, newsletter) | +100 / week combined |
| Engagement | Comments per post, saves | 10+ comments |
| Leads | Profile visits, DMs, lead-magnet signups | 5+ qualified conversations / week |
| Revenue | Calls booked from content, deals won from content | 2+ calls / week |

Focus on **qualified conversations**, not vanity followers. 2,000 of the right followers beat 50,000 random ones.
